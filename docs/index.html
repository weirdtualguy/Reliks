Generative art on Kaspa

The artwork is a program. Its hash lives in the contract.

Reliks anchors a small deterministic Reliks-VM program inside a Kaspa covenant. Anyone can re-render every edition from public chain data or an offline provenance bundle. This page does exactly that in your browser, and withholds the art if any check fails.

Protocol
What Reliks is, and what it is not.


Reliks is three Silverscript covenants, the Reliks-VM deterministic bytecode runtime, and a set of verification tools. It is not a custodial marketplace, an indexer or an audited product. This page keeps the claims small enough to check.
